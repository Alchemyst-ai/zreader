This chapter is MDX, with `content.math: "katex"`. Inline: $E = mc^2$ and $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$. Braces between dollar signs are TeX, not JavaScript: $\frac{a}{b}$.

$$
\int_0^1 x^2 \, dx = \frac{1}{3}
$$

Highlight a formula and quote it to the scratchpad: it arrives as TeX and renders there too. In the pad, type `$x^2$` or `$$` and a space for a formula, or use the Σ button.

</Callout>
