This chapter is an MDX file compiled by Vite. Select any sentence to **highlight** it, add a note, or quote it into the scratchpad. Everything you do is saved in this browser.

<Callout title="Try it">
  Switch on highlight mode in the top bar, then tap a sentence. Open **Aa** to change the theme, text size and font.
</Callout>

## Where things live

- The catalogue (`src/books/index.ts`) is built from `book.json` files and the chapters next to them.
- Routing is React Router; the reader uses its `Link` through `ReaderProvider`.
- Styling comes from your shadcn tokens via `zreader/tailwind.css`.

## Bookmarks and progress

Press the bookmark button to remember the block at the top of the screen. Scroll to the end and the chapter is marked as read on the book page.
