Images in Markdown syntax go through zreader's `rehypeImages` at build time: they are measured, lazy-loaded and, when they have a title, wrapped in a figure with a caption.

![A bar chart](/images/diagram.png "A file from public/, measured at build time. Tap it to open the viewer.")

Inline images stay in the line, like this dot ![a blue dot](data:image/png;base64,iVBORw0KGgoAAAANSUhEUgAAAAoAAAAKCAYAAACNMs+9AAAAFklEQVR42mNkYPhfz0AEYBxVSF+FAP5FDvcfRYWgAAAAAElFTkSuQmCC), which is a base64 data URI.

## The viewer

Tap the chart, or focus it with the keyboard and press Enter. Pinch, scroll or double-tap to zoom; drag to pan; swipe down or press Escape to close.

## Images in your notes

Open the scratchpad and paste or drop an image. It's stored in IndexedDB and included in backups (Aa → Export backup).
